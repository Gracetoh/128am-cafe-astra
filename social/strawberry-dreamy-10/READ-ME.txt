128am Cafe | Dreamy strawberry matcha collection
10 new portrait social images, each in 3:4 format.

Creative brief
Dreamy strawberry-matcha cafe imagery in blush, cream and olive. Soft natural
product photography with creamy textures, paired with oversized editorial serif
lettering and delicate italic accents. Minimal, nostalgic and appetising, with a
playful personality. Soft light and reduced prominent condensation keep the cup
from looking overly sharp.

Contents

Product details
Strawberry Matcha Latte, pink strawberry cold foam, green matcha and milk,
strawberry syrup base, short clear sundae cup and 128am Cafe label.
Finished drink views use a dome lid. Image 05 is a preparation scene with an
open cup so that the foam can be poured naturally.

Method
Created using the built-in image generation tool, using the original drink
photograph and approved softer brand art as references. These are styled
generated images, not unedited photographs. Typography is rendered lettering,
not a supplied font file. No previous collections were overwritten.
