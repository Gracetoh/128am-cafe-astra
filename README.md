# 128am Cafe creative library

Product images, social-media collections, galleries and downloadable packs for 128am Cafe.

The collections are organised under `social/`. Each collection keeps its individual images and its original supporting files together. Where available, open `gallery.html` after downloading the folder to compare designs and download individual images.

These are AI-generated marketing assets based on the cafe's product photographs. Generation prompts are included where available.

## Latest collections

- [Strawberry favourites: 25 images](social/strawberry-favourites-25/)
- [Matcha collection: 25 images](social/matcha-reference-25/)
- [Strawberry reference collection: 5 images](social/strawberry-reference-5/)

`asset-manifest.json` records the relative path, byte size and SHA-256 checksum of every uploaded collection file.
